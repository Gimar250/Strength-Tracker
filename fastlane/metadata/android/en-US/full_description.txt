A minimalist, privacy-focused workout tracking app.

Build routines with exercises, sets and rest timers, then log the weight and reps of every set while you work out. All data is stored locally on the device — no account, no cloud, no tracking.

Features:

- Routines with rep-based or timed exercises
- Rest timer with beeps, configurable in settings
- Workout history with per-exercise progression
- Session details for the last 14 days
- CSV export and import
- Dark and light themes, adjustable font size
